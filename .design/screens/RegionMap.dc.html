Real geometry, not a drawing. Mainland outlines are Natural Earth 1:110m, the island states and the context states are Natural Earth 1:50m, all on one Mercator projection fitted to the region. Comoros and Mauritius are enlarged into their own panels because their true extent is smaller than one pixel at regional scale, and both are styled, selected and reached by keyboard exactly as the mainland twelve are. Labels are an HTML layer over the geometry, so they scale with the map and stay legible at 375px.
