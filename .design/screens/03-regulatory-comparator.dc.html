How a missing value is shown. Where a member state has not reported an indicator, the cell reads Not reported with a dashed marker, and the row keeps its height. Comoros in the Roaming and Mobile category is the live example: select Comoros to see it. A blank cell would be indistinguishable from a value of zero, so no cell is ever left blank.
APPENDIX

The wide table at 375px

A four-column comparison cannot be a table on a phone. Below 720px the comparison transposes: one card per indicator, member states stacked inside it as labelled rows. Every value keeps its provenance marker and its source line. Nothing is hidden behind a horizontal scroll, and nothing requires a hover.
