Confidentiality, and how it is drawn

PublicVisible without signing in. Outline lock, no fill.
MemberAny signed-in member state representative. Navy fill.
ConfidentialNamed committee members only. Red fill, red row tint, locked padlock icon.
SecretariatSecretariat staff only. Deepest red, never listed to other users.
Each level pairs a colour with a text label and a distinct icon, so classification is legible in greyscale, in print and to a screen reader.
