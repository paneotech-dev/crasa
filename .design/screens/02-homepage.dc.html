Automatic rotation and WCAG 2.1 AA, held together

CRASA asked for a rotating hero and has separately made WCAG 2.1 AA a condition of acceptance. Success criterion 2.2.2, Pause Stop Hide, requires a pause mechanism for anything that moves automatically for more than five seconds. Both requests are met rather than traded off.

  • Rotation runs at eight seconds a slide and pauses on hover and whenever keyboard focus enters the region.
  • The pause control is visible at all times, not revealed on hover. Once pressed, rotation stays off for the session.
  • Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce there is no automatic rotation at all. The first slide holds and the controls remain.
  • Indicators are real buttons carrying aria-current, numbered so position and count are legible without colour.
  • Slide changes are announced through a polite live region, so a screen reader is informed once rather than flooded.
  • The key figures count up once on entry, then hold. Under the reduced preference they render at their final value.

Accessibility, motion

The reduced-motion variant of this screen

Reduced motion removes movement, not feedback. Position and scale stop changing; opacity and colour transitions stay, so every state change is still visible. The preference is honoured live on this page, not simulated. Both demonstrations run once on load, in line with the motion specification, which forbids looping decoration. Reload to replay them.

What is removed: travel, scale, parallax, count-up, and the reveal stagger. The homepage and the responsive screen both carry this variant.
