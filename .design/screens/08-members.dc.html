Botswana Communications Regulatory Authority

BOCRA
Visit bocra.org.bw

Mandate

BOCRA regulates the communications sector in Botswana, covering telecommunications, internet and data services, broadcasting, postal services and radio spectrum. It licenses operators, monitors quality of service, protects consumers, and manages the national numbering and frequency plans.

Within CRASA, BOCRA sits on all six committees and hosts the Secretariat, which is based in Gaborone.

Language handling

Authority names stay in their own language in every interface. INACOM and INCM keep their Portuguese names, ANRTIC and ARPTC keep their French names, and each carries a lang attribute so a screen reader pronounces it correctly. A translation is offered in parentheses only where the authority publishes one itself.
