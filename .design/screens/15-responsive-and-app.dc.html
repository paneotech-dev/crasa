What is cached, and what is not

Cached. The interface shell in all three languages, your agendas, and any document you explicitly save.
Never cached. Documents graded Confidential or Secretariat. They require a live authenticated session, so they cannot be left on a device that is later lost.
Stale-aware. Every cached page states when it was last synchronised, so a delegate never quotes a superseded revision by accident.
Accessibility, motion

The reduced-motion variant of this screen

Reduced motion removes movement, not feedback. Position and scale stop changing; opacity and colour transitions stay, so every state change is still visible. The preference is honoured live on this page, not simulated. Both demonstrations run once on load, in line with the motion specification, which forbids looping decoration. Reload to replay them.
