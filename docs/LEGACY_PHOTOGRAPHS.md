to the crawl; do not edit by hand.*

## The hero recommendation

**None of them. Keep the supplied hero.**

Screen 02's band wants a landscape photograph of at least 1920 across, an
institutional subject, and a quiet third at one end for the headline to sit
over. Two of the sixty-five clear the measurements. Both were opened and
neither works, for reasons no size check can see:

- **`IMG_1992.JPG`** (3984&times;2656) — CRASA Postal Committee Advances Regional Resilience and Cooperation
- **`IMG_2143.jpg`** (4032&times;2268) — CRASA courtesy call on BEREC

The first is one speaker at a lectern, filmed centre-frame, with a CRAN pull-up
banner filling the left third. CRAN is one of the fourteen. A regional
association whose homepage opens on one member state's branding is making a
claim it does not mean to make, and the headline would have to sit on top of
that banner because it is the only clear area.

The second is a delegation line-up in BEREC's lobby in Europe, under the
European sign and beside an EU flag. IMAGE_BRIEF already holds two supplied
images for being photographed outside the region; this is the same problem and
the EU emblem makes it worse, because CLAUDE.md forbids showing that emblem
until CRASA supplies the wording that goes with it.

What the set is good for is the institutional slot and article heroes, which is
where it is short. Twenty-four photographs of CRASA meetings, signings and
courtesy calls is more institutional material than the platform has had.

*This paragraph is a judgement about what the pictures show, not a
measurement. If the crawl is re-run and the candidates change, somebody has to
open them again.*
